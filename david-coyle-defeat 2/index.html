THE STORY

What started as a standard bout quickly turned into one of the messiest moments in modern combat sports history.

The Man

David Coyle entered the ring that night with confidence, red shorts, white gloves, and a name stitched proudly across his waistband. Fans expected a tough, physical fight. Nobody expected what came next.

The Green Disaster

In a sequence still debated by fans, Coyle found himself on his back, covered head-to-toe in a thick, glowing green substance. Whether it was a failed special-effects gag, an overzealous prank, or pure chaos, the result was the same: total defeat by slime.

The Image That Defined a Career

The photo of Coyle flat on the canvas, eyes closed, body glistening under the arena lights and coated in neon green, became an instant classic. It captured everything — exhaustion, surprise, and the pure absurdity of the moment.
